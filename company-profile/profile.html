India generates over 1.7 million tonnes of electronic waste every year, and less than a third reaches a formal recycling channel. The rest is burned, buried or broken apart by hand.

Advait Green Recycling exists to change that ratio. We are a GPCB authorised recycler operating an integrated facility at Vamaj, Mahesana, processing electronic, plastic and battery waste through mechanical and chemical recovery lines.

The name Advait means “not two” — industry and environment are not opposing interests. A recycler that isn't commercially sound won't survive to protect anything; one that isn't environmentally rigorous doesn't deserve to.

Our position

Environmental compliance, data security and material recovery usually get treated as three separate problems. We treat them as one — one partner, one documentation trail, one accountable contact from collection to certificate.

01 · The discipline

What waste management is

Collecting, transporting, treating and recovering material that has reached the end of its first useful life — safely, legally, and with maximum value returned to the economy.

02 · The arithmetic

Why recovery matters

Recycling one tonne of circuit boards recovers more gold than 17 tonnes of mined ore. Recovery isn't charity for the planet — it is the cheaper, cleaner and increasingly the only legal way to source secondary raw material.

03 · The return

The business case

Beyond compliance, recycling reduces landfill liability, protects brand reputation, unlocks scrap value from written-off assets, and delivers documented evidence for ESG and CSR reporting.

Recover the value. Retire the liability.

Service 03
extended producer
responsibility

The compliance is mandatory. The complexity doesn't have to be yours.

If you manufacture, import or brand a product that eventually becomes electronic, plastic or battery waste, EPR obligations apply to you — and the filing requirements have only tightened. We act as your compliance partner end to end.

Who this is for

ProducersManufacturersImporters Brand ownersE-commerce sellers RefurbishersBulk consumers

Filing deadlines are not negotiable.

Quarterly returns and annual reconciliation carry penalties for late or unsupported submission. We track your calendar and file against evidence we have physically collected.
