01  /  Who We Are

A Recycling Company Built for
India's Next Decade of Waste

India generates over 1.7 million tonnes of electronic waste every year, and less than a third of it reaches a formal recycling channel. The rest is burned, buried or broken apart by hand.

Advait Green Recycling exists to change that ratio.

We are a GPCB authorised recycler operating an integrated facility at Location, Gujarat, processing electronic waste, plastic waste, battery waste and industrial scrap through mechanical and chemical recovery lines. Our work sits at the intersection of three outcomes that usually get treated separately — environmental compliance, data security, and material recovery.

For our clients, that means one partner, one documentation trail, and one accountable point of contact from collection to certificate.

The name Advait means “not two” — the idea that industry and environment are not opposing interests. A recycling business that isn't commercially sound will not survive to protect anything; one that isn't environmentally rigorous doesn't deserve to.

01 · THE DISCIPLINE

What Waste Management Is

Collecting, transporting, treating and recovering material that has reached the end of its first useful life — safely, legally, and with the maximum possible value returned to the economy.

02 · THE ARITHMETIC

Why Recovery Matters

Recycling one tonne of circuit boards recovers more gold than 17 tonnes of mined ore. Recovery isn't charity for the planet — it is the cheaper, cleaner and increasingly the only legal way to source secondary raw material.

03 · THE RETURN

The Business Case

Beyond compliance, recycling reduces landfill liability, protects brand reputation, unlocks scrap value from written-off assets, and delivers the documented evidence your ESG and CSR reporting now demands.

Service 01

E-Waste Recycling
& Management

Recover the value. Retire the liability.

Electronic waste is the fastest-growing waste stream in the country, and the most legally sensitive to dispose of carelessly. Under the E-Waste (Management) Rules, the generator's responsibility does not end at handover — it ends at documented, authorised treatment. We take over that entire chain.

What we handle

  • IT equipment — desktops, laptops, servers, monitors, printers, networking hardware
  • Consumer electronics — televisions, air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines
  • Telecom infrastructure — switches, routers, base station equipment, cabling
  • Industrial electronics — control panels, PLCs, instrumentation, UPS systems
  • Medical electronics if authorised
  • Mobile devices, tablets, accessories and batteries

05  /  Our Process

Gate to Certificate

Five stages, each with its own record. The documentation isn't produced at the end — it accumulates as the consignment moves.

01

Assess

We audit your waste stream, categorise it under the applicable rules, and quote against actual recoverable value.

Record produced

Waste audit report · Category sheet · Recovery-based quotation

02

Collect

Sealed, GPS-tracked transport under a valid manifest. Weighment recorded at pickup and at gate entry.

Record produced

Movement manifest · Pickup weighment slip · Transport log

03

Sort & Dismantle

Manual de-manufacturing followed by mechanical segregation into ferrous, non-ferrous, plastics, glass, PCB and hazardous fractions.

Record produced

Gate-entry weighbridge slip · Serial-wise teardown list · Photographic record

04

Recover

Shredding, density separation and metal recovery lines return clean secondary raw material to industry.

Record produced

Fraction-wise yield record · Downstream channelling documents

05

Certify

Certificate of Recycling, Certificate of Data Destruction where applicable, and quarterly EPR filing support.

Record produced

Certificate of Recycling · Certificate of Data Destruction · Form-wise records
