Executive summary
India's formal recycling gap is a compliance liability for waste generators — Advait Green closes it with an integrated, fully documented recovery chain
01 · The situation

Volume is rising faster than formal capacity

  • India generates over 1.7 million tonnes of e-waste annually
  • Less than a third reaches a formal recycling channel
  • The balance is burned, buried or dismantled by hand
  • E-waste, plastic and battery rules now place the obligation on the generator and the producer, not the handler
02 · The complication

Compliance is fragmented across vendors

  • Most organisations run three separate vendors — IT disposal, plastic, compliance filing
  • Three documentation sets that do not reconcile at audit
  • Liability does not transfer unless treatment is authorised and evidenced
  • Retired media carries live data risk under the DPDP Act
03 · The resolution

One authorised partner, one evidence trail

  • GPCB authorised integrated facility at Vamaj, Mahesana
  • Six service lines under one accountable contact
  • Documentation accumulates as the consignment moves, not at the end
  • Certificates, manifests and Form-wise records issued as standard
So what Consolidating e-waste, plastic, battery and EPR filing with a single authorised recycler removes the reconciliation gap that fails audits — and converts a recurring compliance cost into a documented, recoverable-value transaction.
Source: E-Waste (Management) Rules; Plastic Waste Management Rules; Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022; company data

The residual two-thirds is dismantled by hand, acid-leached or landfilled. That is a public health problem — and, for the organisation whose asset tag is still on the device, an unextinguished liability.

Exhibit 2 · Where the obligation now sits
RuleWho carries the duty
E-Waste (Management) Rules Generator's responsibility ends only at documented, authorised treatment
Plastic Waste Management Rules Brand owners and importers must meet category-wise recycling targets
Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022 Producers accountable for collection and refurbishment targets
DPDP Act Data on retired media remains the holder's exposure until destroyed

Why this matters commercially

Recycling one tonne of circuit boards recovers more gold than 17 tonnes of mined ore. Recovery is not environmental charity — it is the cheaper, cleaner and increasingly the only legal route to secondary raw material.

So what Because the duty attaches to the generator and the producer rather than the collector, choosing an unauthorised or undocumented handler does not reduce exposure — it defers and compounds it.
